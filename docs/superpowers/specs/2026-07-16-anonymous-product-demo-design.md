# Anonymous product demo design

## Goal

Publish a concise, anonymous product demonstration that explains how Alomat turns approved source signals into traceable, human-approved publishing packages. The demo must strengthen the managed SaaS story without naming a customer, exposing private interfaces, or inventing performance claims.

## Deliverables

- A 60-second, caption-led 16:9 MP4 suitable for the website and social sharing.
- A canonical `/demo` page with a native video player, accessible transcript, product boundaries, and pilot conversion path.
- Shared, typed scene copy so the video, transcript, and tests describe the same workflow.
- Homepage, platform, case-study, sitemap, and `llms.txt` discovery paths.

## Narrative

1. The problem: important signals arrive across disconnected sources.
2. Monitor: only approved sources enter the workflow.
3. Verify: related coverage, duplicates, and source traces are preserved.
4. Understand: editorial memory and rules provide context.
5. Produce: specialist agents assemble a channel-ready package.
6. Review: a human editor approves, edits, rejects, or escalates.
7. Learn: decisions become evaluation examples without removing human authority.
8. Close: start with one real publishing workflow.

## Visual system

Use Alomat's editorial palette, large typography, strong whitespace, and one focal idea per scene. Product activity is shown as abstract operational cards and source traces rather than customer screenshots. Every readable element stays inside a generous video-safe area.

## Claim and privacy boundary

- No customer, channel, person, country, or private project name.
- No unsupported metrics, accuracy percentages, time savings, or autonomous-publication claims.
- Explicitly state that agents prepare and editors decide.
- Describe the demo as a representative product walkthrough based on an implemented workflow, not a live customer screen recording.

## Accessibility and delivery

- The video works without audio and includes all narrative text on screen.
- The web page includes a full transcript and reduced-motion-safe native controls.
- MP4 metadata and a `VideoObject` schema make the asset understandable to search systems.
